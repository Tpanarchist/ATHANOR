# ATHANOR — roadmap

## 001: Operational cornerstone — implemented

Typed structural knowledge, exact procedures, recursive composition, structured
teaching, atomic validation, provenance, and restore are operational. The original
61 correctness tests remain the foundation's invariants. Definitions and executable
knowledge are addressable structural participants. This does not prove the architecture's
general-intelligence hypothesis or establish external truth.

## 002: First neural observer — implemented

Shared structural encoding, sparse learned associations, a reused recurrent process,
operation/argument pointers, learned abstention, persistent activation, operational
self-map, separate comparison channels, supervised optimization and checkpoints.
New installed structures/procedures use the same parameters. Exact execution remains
in Goal 001. Evidence is bounded held-out proposal learning, real kernel feedback,
explicit preference correction, post-training teaching, and resumed computation/training.
See NEURAL_OBSERVER and VALIDATION for results and remaining binding errors.

## 003: Meaning, teaching and grounding — implemented

Bounded language observations connect attributed expression conventions to native
concepts/operations and observed participants. Shared recurrent learned meaning,
operation and ordered argument selection; explicit ambiguity/conflict/unknown states;
atomic validated teaching; specialization; adopted corrections retaining old evidence;
offline lesson preparation/import; scoped observation support; measured recognition
and capability history; learned training and checkpoint continuation. Two-family
operational transfer is exercised with newly installed concepts and fixed weights.
See MEANING, MEANING_API and VALIDATION. This is bounded operational meaning, not
open-domain language understanding or learned sensory perception.

## 004: Grounded observer — implemented

Independent persistent bounded 3D territory uses the native cornerstone; a spherical
body receives actual raw stereo images and limited proprioception. Learned shared
perception/fusion feeds the existing relational recurrent observer, which proposes
native validated motor procedures and predicts measured consequences. Camera-grounded
teaching, conservative identity/depth hypotheses, occlusion, independent motion,
active sensing, physical failures, world/self/interface alternatives, empirical
knowledge consolidation, replay, complete-state checkpoints and a live local viewer
operate through one runtime. Exact geometry remains exact; belief remains qualified.
See GROUNDED_OBSERVER, GROUNDED_API and VALIDATION. The original language and
mathematical/categorical paths remain available. This is bounded embodied learning,
not a general learned causal theory or full digital universe.

## 005: Autonomous discovery and continual learning — implemented

Bounded model-ranked inquiry, native alternative hypotheses and evidence revisions,
conditional executable knowledge from untaught consequences, transfer checks, dynamic
capability/interface feedback, periodic shared parameter updates, selective indexed
activation, viewer/headless controls and optimizer/checkpoint continuation. See
AUTONOMOUS_DISCOVERY, DISCOVERY_API and DISCOVERY_TRAINING. Initial induction covers zero
and conditional constant effects. Arbitrary nonlinear laws, calibrated uncertainty,
authenticated external observations and general interference guarantees remain future work.

## 006: Scalable activation and knowledge retention

Measure total encoding, inference, training and knowledge storage costs. Add graph
indexes, learned sparse activation budgets with explicit exclusion semantics, durable
transactional storage, and dependency-aware history retention against measured workloads.
Avoid silent revision drift or deleting provenance dependencies. Improve representation
fidelity beyond compressed hash features where evidence warrants it. Runtime limits
remain separate from model-size hypotheses.

## 007: Controlled process refinement

Represent procedure/representation proposals as structural data. Validate adoption
against held-out execution, retention and resource contracts, with rollback and
versioned usage. Review changes to the trusted native validator/interpreter separately.
The cornerstone makes operations reflectable; the neural observer can select them.
Neither milestone implements autonomous architecture rewriting.

## 008: Broader digital-universe integration

Extend the local Goal 004 simulation to richer environments and explicit queued
actions, observations and effect receipts. Preserve environment/kernel boundaries,
headless operation, reproducible replay and resource accounting. Evaluate agency,
transfer, recovery and persistent learning after those interfaces exist.

Each milestone needs contracts and working evidence before being called implemented.
Open-domain chat, large ingestion pipelines, autonomous architecture search, elaborate
visuals and comparative benchmark campaigns remain outside the first four goals.
