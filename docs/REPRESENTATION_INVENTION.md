# ADR-008: Executable interpretations and measured representation revision

**Status:** Accepted for implementation under Goal 008  
**Date:** 2026-10-11  
**Deciders:** User milestone and implementation contracts

## Context

Inspection of 0.7.0 finds immutable canonical values, revision-pinned native procedures,
atomic recursive execution, learned structural pointers, shared recurrent activation,
semantic teaching, RGB foreground/appearance features, grounded inquiry, process
composition, and structural induction. Numerical fields and raw camera measurements are
supplied; appearance features and procedure priorities are learned. Connectivity and
raster adjacency are installed algorithms. Concept predicates use a fixed grammar over
observed fields and degrees. Concepts and procedures compose, but raster-to-graph is
selected by input family rather than evidence. No first-class representation contract
connects competing interpretations, task utility, revisions and reusable fragments.

## Decision

Add a representation front end owned by GroundedObserver. It constructs native unary
procedures through Processes.structural_call and uses Induction's shared neural ranking,
retention training, execution, goals and revision channels. It has no alternative
interpreter, environment authority or separate neural network.

New exact algebra operations construct a directed relation from a scoped two-role
predicate, expose induced component graphs, flatten collections and reduce exact
numerical measurements. These are necessary to construct relationships, reopen groups,
and derive relative properties; none encode a complete problem solution. Centering,
scaling, feature projection, ordering and composed interpretations are constructed from
record/map/arithmetic/call expressions, rather than installed whole transformations.

Observations, executable representations, and interpretation receipts are distinct native
participants. Receipts reference original observations and typed execution outputs.
Immutable observation/representation pairs can reuse their pinned result, with explicit
reuse receipts. Raw camera bytes and learned foreground measurements have separate
provenance. Foreground pixels receive neither object identities nor authoritative groups.

Evidence compares predictions of an observable relationship between pairs of inputs;
positive and negative outcomes qualify representations. An equality-only collapse cannot
justify general discrimination. Multiple consistent interpretations remain separate;
their disagreement returns unknown. Search is bounded, checks structural compatibility
before installing/executing candidates, and lets the recurrent scorer determine proposal
and beam order. Measured partial discrepancies guide refinement without counting as
successful final predictions.

Representations can supply interpretations to concept formation, transformation induction
and the existing goal solver. A failed active interpretation is recorded before bounded
revision. Successful representations and repeated procedure fragments become native
knowledge with inspectable dependencies, source observations, revisions and limitations.
Contradictions qualify dependent knowledge, preserving historical pinned definitions.

## Options considered and consequences

Expanding 0.7's fixed concept grammar alone would leave interpretation selection supplied
by the interface. A separate visual solver would violate common execution and learning.
Native compositional interpretations preserve contracts and reuse current learning, at
the cost of finite proposal operators, measured perception limits and bounded search.
Compression is justified by reusable execution receipts and shared fragments, not by
assuming a shorter representation is true. Exact computation, native knowledge, learned
parameters, active interpretations and actual observations remain separate.

Implementation and validation must cover native algorithms, evidential ambiguity,
counterexamples, caching, dependencies, neural selection, camera isolation, restoration,
headless and viewer commands, cross-interface transfer and Goals 001–007 preservation.
No ARC data, examples, solutions, derived tasks or benchmark techniques are used.
