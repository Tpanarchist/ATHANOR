# Inquiry teaching, adaptation and consolidation

`train_inquiry(observer, steps=1600, seed=5005)` teaches general experimental priorities
to the existing shared scorer. Synthetic lessons contain uncertainty, relevance, expected
distinguishing benefit, reliability, cost and risk, including abstention. They contain no
object mobility, territory coefficients, hidden identities or discovery-world examples.
Threshold distinctions/scaled numbers improve the existing compressed representation.
No new neural parameter module is added.

Initial priority teaching optimizes the shared scorer while keeping the encoder/recurrent
parameters fixed. Distillation retains original operation, ordered argument and meaning
scores on native structural/semantic candidate/context pairs and the current grounded
frame. `ModelOutput.score_trace` is transient training instrumentation. This prepares a
general competence; environmental acquisition subsequently happens through model-selected
actions, restricted consequences, native hypothesis revision and procedure construction.

Fast adaptation updates map, active hypotheses, capability reliability and interface
comparisons immediately. Knowledge consolidation requires repeated evidence/passive controls
and compiles conditional predictions with measured coefficients. Equivalent estimates reuse
their existing procedure identities. Contradictory evidence is retained and current reuse
withheld. Unknown sensing is never converted into a negative fact.

Slow neural consolidation reuses `GroundedTrainer` and its AdamW optimizer every four
autonomous actions. It learns physical readouts from real one-step consequences and applies
a small measured information-benefit preference to inquiry scores. Proposal distillation
and bounded per-update drift protect earlier behavior; this is not a general long-term
interference guarantee. The checkpoint saves optimizer moments, RNG, counters, trainability
and original pending frame/prior. Pure activation/native changes do not imply weight changes.

The continuous acceptance session configures territory response 0.12, absent from the
earlier 0.2-response curriculum, then supplies only restricted camera/body observations.
The neural observer selects passive controls and interactions, fits the camera effect,
compiles a native prediction, and checks it in a changed arrangement. It continues a new
capability inquiry, updates shared parameters, saves/restores and crosses another optimizer
boundary with matching decisions/parameters. Established structural and semantic proposals
are checked before priority teaching, after teaching and after continual updates.

Focused tests cover provisional single samples, passive comparisons, contradictory evidence,
excluded sensing, conditional native execution, budgets, abstention, hidden-input isolation,
inspection purity, index behavior and restoration. This is functional validation rather
than a comparative architecture benchmark. Priority accuracy is reported separately from
discovery success and has no truth interpretation.

Initial induction covers zero/conditional constant effects. Arbitrary nonlinear law
discovery, general diagnostic planning, calibrated uncertainty, indistinguishable identities,
universal causality and long-term retention guarantees remain outside this implementation.
