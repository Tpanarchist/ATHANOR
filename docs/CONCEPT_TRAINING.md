# Shared learning and independent Goal 007 validation

There is one recurrent observer and one owned optimizer. The structural front end uses
the same Candidate representation and typed unary composition constructor as process
discovery. Native structural hypothesis expressions, operands, dependencies and evidence
enter the existing encoder and recurrent graph. Neural score features include complexity,
measured discrepancy, uncertainty and structural expression activation. The shared scorer
prioritizes concept proposals, prunes candidate compositions, selects a rule, ranks goal
transitions and chooses informative tests before their outcomes are obtained.

Execution evaluates candidates exactly through the kernel. A neural first choice must
pass its evidence contract; the system does not silently substitute a symbolic winner.
Multiple fitting hypotheses remain available. Tests explicitly force an unsupported neural
first choice and verify abstention. Revision from new consequences can replace a previously
preferred explanation while retaining its historical evidence and rejecting its reuse.

Training consolidates those measured score targets through `Processes.train_priority`,
with the owned GroundedTrainer. Only the existing scorer parameters change during this
structural consolidation; no task-specific network or parameter block is allocated.
Retained inquiry/process scores and earlier structural/semantic proposal anchors constrain
the update. This preserves earlier learned activation while allowing consequence-driven
priority learning. It does not claim every encoder/GRU weight trains during this session.
World consequence and prior supervised/semantic learning retain their original mechanisms.

Compact checkpoint JSON retains every native entry and digest within the unchanged
256 MiB member budget; serialization whitespace does not consume that budget.

Active reasoning roots are limited to relevant native hypotheses or acquired rules, rather
than every installed generated candidate. Full audit history remains stored. The existing
512-node graph budget and recurrent/self-map history limits are preserved. A subsequent
world observation restores the ordinary motor-operation scope. Problem reasoning cannot
grant new authority to physical actions or bypass the native validator.

Concept proposal currently composes observed field equality/difference, ordered peer
relations, relational degree and connectivity. Exact algorithms are supplied; their useful
composition and selection are learned from observations and measured outcomes. This is a
finite initial grammar, not invention of new mathematical primitives or unrestricted
perceptual ontology. Symbolic rasters are actual raw occupied-cell observations, not camera
object understanding. Unknown memberships and unresolved alternative outputs remain explicit.

## Continuous session

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m athanor.grounded.induction_demo
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest discover -s tests -v
```

`world.situations.ProblemStream` produces independent seeded relational and symbolic spatial
environments. It privately executes a native rule to produce demonstration outcomes. The
observer receives raw values and examples, never that rule, its choice, or privileged groups.
Observed property keys and identities are anonymous. The acceptance does not contain names
for the expected concept or supply a library of whole answers.

The session restores `artifacts/process-observer.athanor` (the actual learned Goal 006
checkpoint), self-directs concept formation from three unfamiliar relational inputs,
constructs competing native rules and tests a fresh input. It composes the acquired
distinction with another concept. Three raw raster examples support a different spatial
transformation. Another raster context reuses the same relational concept through native
representation adapters. A symmetric single example retains uncertainty. A goal then
requires both the learned eligible group and property ordering, with three distinct later
inputs qualifying the generalized plan. Saving/restoring preserves native knowledge,
optimizer and recurrent state; another fresh objective is solved after restoration.

The session measures prior structural/semantic proposal accuracy before and after and
executes the Goal 006 recursive process again. Ordinary tests check exact continuation of
the next neural update, active-query restoration, malformed checkpoint rejection, native
atomic failure, typed role scope, directed order, relational substitution, uncertainty,
revision and dependency qualification. A separate installed wheel check covers portability.

Generated artifacts include `concept-observer.athanor`, `concept-observer.report.json`,
`concept-run.log`, `goal007-contracts.log`, and `goal007-regression.log`. The measured report
records candidates, qualified alternatives, dependencies, actual held-out results, goal
depth, parameter changes, prior-capability retention and restart outcomes.

No official ARC datasets, published ARC examples, solutions, benchmark heuristics or
comparative campaign are used. ARC remains reserved for explicitly authorized later work.
